Who We Are

Your Trusted Home Service Partner

Regular Solution began with a simple frustration shared by every household in Chennai: finding a dependable person for a small repair often takes longer than the repair itself. We built one platform where families can reach verified professionals for any job around the home — quickly, at a fair price, and without the guesswork.

Today our network covers sixteen service categories, from air conditioner maintenance and deep cleaning to painting, carpentry, shifting and interior work. Every professional is identity-checked and skill-assessed before their first booking, and rated by customers after every job.

  • Background-verified and trained professionals
  • Written quotes approved before work starts
  • Available all seven days, 24 hours a day
  • One helpline for booking, updates and support
