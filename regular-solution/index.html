Pricing

Factors Affecting Moving Charges

The cost of a move varies according to your requirements. Main factors include:

  • Size of your home and quantity of goods.
  • Distance between pickup and destination.
  • Packing materials and level of packing required.
  • Floor level, lift access and carrying distance.
  • Vehicle and workforce requirements.
  • Additional services such as unpacking or furniture assembly.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book packers and movers?

Call Regular Solution at 8925516635 or submit the inquiry form with your pickup location, destination, moving date and household inventory.

How much do packers and movers charge?

The price depends on the size of the move, distance, packing requirements, access conditions and additional services. Request a customized estimate.

Do you provide 1BHK, 2BHK and 3BHK house shifting?

Request an estimate for your house size and confirm availability and service scope before booking.

Can I book local house shifting?

Share your pickup and destination localities to confirm availability and receive a quote.

Do you provide office relocation services?

Contact the team with details of your office furniture, equipment, locations and preferred schedule to confirm available services.

Are packing materials included in the quotation?

Packing materials depend on the selected package. Ask for a quotation that states which materials and packing services are included.

How early should I book my move?

Contact the team as early as possible, especially for preferred dates or larger relocations. Confirm availability before booking.

How can I get a moving quote?

Call 8925516635 or fill in the quote form with your moving route, service type and preferred date, verify your mobile number with the OTP, and submit.

Does Regular Solution carry out the move directly?

Regular Solution is an enquiry and connection platform. We pass your requirement to moving service providers, and the move itself is carried out by the provider you agree terms with.

Can I enquire about local and intercity shifting?

Yes. Enter both the pickup and the destination area in the form — for a local move or between two cities — and mention any special requirements in the additional details box.

How is the moving price calculated?

Pricing depends on the volume of items, distance, floor and lift access, packing material and the date of the move. The provider confirms the final price with you after reviewing your requirement.
