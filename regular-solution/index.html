Factors Affecting Moving Charges

The cost of a move varies according to your requirements. Main factors include:

  • Size of your home and quantity of goods.
  • Distance between pickup and destination.
  • Packing materials and level of packing required.
  • Floor level, lift access and carrying distance.
  • Vehicle and workforce requirements.
  • Additional services such as unpacking or furniture assembly.
