FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get a quote for packers and movers in Chennai?

Fill in the form on this page with your moving route, service type and preferred date, verify your mobile number with the OTP, and submit. You can also call 1800 419 9090 to share the same details over the phone.

Does Regular Solution carry out the move directly?

Regular Solution is an enquiry and connection platform. We pass your requirement to moving service providers, and the move itself is carried out by the provider you agree terms with.

Can I enquire about local and intercity shifting?

Yes. Enter both the pickup and the destination area in the form — within Chennai or between Chennai and another city — and mention any special requirements in the additional details box.

How is the moving price calculated?

Pricing depends on the volume of items, distance, floor and lift access, packing material and the date of the move. The provider confirms the final price with you after reviewing your requirement.

Can I call instead of filling out the form?

Yes. Call 1800 419 9090 and share your moving details with our coordinator, who will record the same information for you.
