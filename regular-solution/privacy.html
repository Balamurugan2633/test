Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Who we are

Regular Solution ("we", "us") operates this website as a platform that collects home service and moving enquiries and connects them with service providers. Our registered address is No.31/12, Avvai Nagar, Thiruvanmiyur, Chennai – 600041.

2. What we collect

When you use an enquiry or booking form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name, mobile number and, where you provide it, your email address
  • The service you are enquiring about, your preferred date and time
  • Your pickup and destination areas or service address
  • Any additional details you type into the message field
  • Your IP address and the time the enquiry was received

We send a one-time password (OTP) to the mobile number you enter so that we can confirm the number belongs to you before the enquiry is accepted.

3. How we use it

  • To contact you about the enquiry you submitted
  • To pass your requirement to service providers who can carry out the work
  • To verify your mobile number and prevent spam submissions
  • To keep a record of enquiries for our own service and support purposes

We do not sell your personal details.

4. Who we share it with

We share the details of your enquiry with the service providers who may fulfil it, and with the SMS provider used to deliver your one-time password. Providers are expected to use your details only to respond to your enquiry.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are retained for as long as needed to provide and support the service, and to meet any legal or accounting obligation that applies to us.

6. Cookies and analytics

This site uses a session cookie to hold your mobile-number verification between sending the OTP and submitting the form. If advertising or analytics tags are added to a page, those services may also set their own cookies, subject to their own policies.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you, by writing to info@example.com or calling 1800 419 9090. Please include the mobile number you used so we can find your enquiry.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The revision date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@example.com · 1800 419 9090.
